## 1) Agile in One Page
- **Goal:** Satisfy customers via **early, incremental delivery**.
- **Teams:** **Small, motivated, cross‑functional**; collaborate tightly with customers.
- **Process:** Lightweight, adaptive, continuous feedback; simplicity.
- **When NOT to use:** Very **large projects** needing heavy formal docs/comms; **weak team attitude/skills**.

### Agile vs Plan‑Driven
- **Plan‑Driven:** upfront requirements/design, heavier docs, stable domains, compliance.
- **Agile:** requirements & design **co‑evolve**; respond to change; working software early.

---
## 2) Team Organization
### Why small teams?
- Faster decisions; **fail‑fast → mature faster**; transparency/accountability; familial culture; less social loafing.

### Healthy agile team traits
- Constructive interactions; minimal coordination overhead; visible, meaningful contributions; members flex **beyond rigid role silos**.

### Roles (typical Scrum‑style)
- **Product Owner (PO):** Owns **value**. Defines features, **prioritises** by market value, adjusts per iteration, **accepts/rejects** results, steers **release content/ROI**.
- **Scrum Master (SM):** **Servant‑leader**/**facilitator**. Teaches Scrum, removes **impediments**, fosters collaboration; some PM responsibilities.
- **Developers/Architect/QA/UX:** Build, design, test, and validate increments.

---
## 3) Scrum Essentials
- **Sprint:** Time‑box **1–3 weeks** (≤ 1 month). Produces a **potentially shippable increment**.
- **Artefacts:** **Product backlog**, **Sprint backlog**, **Burndown chart**.
- **Ceremonies:**
  - **Sprint Planning:** Select goals/stories; break into tasks; capacity alignment.
  - **Daily Stand‑up (10–15 min):** Done / Doing / **Blockers**; at least weekly in this course; SM facilitates; record briefly.
  - **Sprint Review:** **Demo** the increment; compare to plan; explain deviations; identify new features.
  - **Retrospective (≤ ~3h):** **What went well? What could improve?** Decide actionable experiments for next sprint.
- **Releases:** Usually **2+ sprints**; release planning sets roadmap, features, milestones.

---
## 4) Project Launch (Kick‑off)
- Clarify **long‑term objectives** and **MVP**.
- Draft initial **user stories**; refine to **tasks**.
- **Triage** product backlog by **value/cost/priority**.

---
## 5) Requirement Gathering
### Preparation
- Survey **existing solutions** & domain context.
- Identify **stakeholders** (users, managers, partner teams, data/API providers).
- Capture **constraints** and any **special tech needs**.

### During sessions
- Use a **clear agenda** and **time‑box** items.
- Employ **visuals** (mockups, user stories, walkthroughs) and **structured techniques**.
- Ask **focused questions** to prioritise (e.g., “Pick **two** for now, which **two** next?”).
- Chair moderates **turn‑taking**; curb dominance; **probe unclear** points; invite quieter voices.

### Managing outcomes
- With permission, **record** meetings.
- Summarise **agreed goals/work items**; create **tracker issues** for new work and follow‑ups.
- **Minute** key discussions/decisions.

### Between meetings
- Maintain **direct channels**; do **periodic demos**; continuously incorporate feedback.
- Schedule **focused sessions** to resolve uncertainties; keep **customer satisfaction** central.

### Negotiating changes
- Evaluate schedule impact; be **honest**; **renegotiate timelines** if needed; factor in **past delays**.

---
## 6) Backlogs & Questions
- **Product Backlog:** Prioritised list of deliverables/features.
- **Sprint Backlog:** Selected items with tasks for the sprint.
- **Good questions:** Specific, bounded, decision‑oriented (avoid “Do you want everything?”).

- Three **Scrum artefacts**?
- Purpose of **Review** vs **Retrospective**?
- One situation **unsuited** for Agile.
- Two responsibilities of **Product Owner**; two of **Scrum Master**.

**Focused prioritisation prompt:**
- “Which **two** features should be implemented **first**, and which **two** **after that**?”

**Kick‑off summary:**
- *Objectives & MVP*, *Initial stories*, *Constraints*, *Risks*, *Backlog triage*, *Next steps*.

---
## 9) Anti‑patterns to avoid
- Agenda without time‑boxes; letting **loudest voice** dominate.
- Purely verbal decisions (no minutes/issues). 
- “Everything is top priority.”
- Long sprints with no demo; skipping retros.
